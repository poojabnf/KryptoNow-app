⚡ Quick Summary

✓ Your private keys and seed phrase are stored only on your device — never transmitted to our servers.
✓ We do not sell your data to any third party.
✓ You can delete your account and data at any time.

KryptoNow ("we", "us", "our") is a non-custodial Web3 wallet application. This Privacy Policy describes how we handle information when you use our iOS app, Android app, and web app at kryptonow.xyz.

By using KryptoNow, you agree to the collection and use of information described in this policy.

🔒 What We Never Collect

🔑 Private keys and seed phrases are generated on your device and stored in your device's Secure Enclave (iOS Keychain / Android Keystore). They are never transmitted to KryptoNow servers or any third party.

  • Your private key or mnemonic seed phrase
  • Transaction amounts or recipients (we read these from public blockchain data only)
  • Browsing history or dApp interactions
  • Location data
  • Contacts or address book

🗄️ Data Storage & Security

  • On-device: Private keys and seed phrases are stored exclusively in your device's hardware-backed secure enclave. We use iOS Keychain and Android Keystore.
  • In transit: All API communication uses HTTPS/TLS 1.3. JWT tokens are signed with a secret key and expire after 7 days.
  • At rest: Our backend database is encrypted at rest. Production secrets are managed via environment variables — not checked into source control.
  • Log retention: Application logs are automatically purged after 15 days.
  • KYC retention: Identity documents are retained for 10 years as required by anti-money-laundering regulations, then securely deleted.

🪪 KYC & Identity Verification

KYC (Know Your Customer) verification is optional unless you choose to use a fiat on-ramp that requires it. KryptoNow uses KYC data solely to comply with applicable financial regulations and to unlock on-ramp functionality.

  • Documents are encrypted in our database and never sold or shared beyond regulatory requirements.
  • You may request deletion of KYC data after the mandatory 10-year AML retention period expires.
  • We do not use KYC data for advertising or profiling.

👶 Children's Privacy

KryptoNow is intended for users who are at least 18 years old (or the minimum legal age in their jurisdiction for financial services). We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can delete it promptly.

⚖️ Your Rights

Depending on your location (EEA, UK, California, India, etc.) you may have the following rights:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction: Update inaccurate information in your profile.
  • Deletion: Delete your account and associated data (except data we are legally required to retain, such as AML-mandated KYC records).
  • Portability: Receive your data in a machine-readable format.
  • Opt-out of notifications: Disable push notifications at any time in Settings → Notifications or in your device settings.

To exercise any of these rights, email us at privacy@kryptonow.xyz. We will respond within 30 days.
